# Project Brief

## Company
**Nexova Solutions** is a human-resources and talent consulting company founded in 2011 with operations described in the canonical company briefing at `/CONTEXT.md`.

Its business spans:
- headhunting / recruitment for managerial and executive profiles;
- outsourced customer-support teams;
- corporate training.

The company also operates Sales/Business Development, Marketing, HR, Technology/Infrastructure and Executive Management functions.

## Current company problem
Nexova has strong domain expertise but weak connected operational infrastructure. Significant recruiting, support, sales, training and executive-reporting work remains manual or fragmented across legacy systems, CRM, spreadsheets and Google Workspace.

## Project objective
Use the 4Geeks AI Engineering milestones to evolve one cumulative Nexova engineering platform rather than create disconnected exercises.

For the current milestone, the concrete objective is narrower:
- make the monorepo agent-ready;
- build the required public website and independent internal backoffice;
- preserve exact academic compliance;
- create only the reusable foundation that demonstrably reduces risk/work in later milestones.

## Product-roadmap status
Historical ideas such as Sales Opportunity, Talent Intelligence, Support Resolution, Learning Advisor and Executive Intelligence are **candidate directions**, not a frozen roadmap. Future milestones must re-evaluate them against current requirements and evidence.

## Success principle
Solve the current Nexova problem first. Generalize only after a stable repeated pattern and evidence of reuse appear.
