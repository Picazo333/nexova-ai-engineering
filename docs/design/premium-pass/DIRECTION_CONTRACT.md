## 1. Design thesis

Nexova is not presented as an AI startup.

It is presented as a mature people-and-services company entering a more intelligent operating era.

The visual system should communicate:

> **human judgment under pressure, disciplined systems, and controlled transformation.**

The evolved Oni Nocturne lineage is the symbolic/art-direction layer for that idea:
- Demon/Oni = force, discipline and controlled danger, expressed through fragmented or partially dissolved form rather than a literal fantasy character;
- darkness = abyssal field, restraint, seriousness and unexposed depth;
- oxblood = signal/action, not ambient decoration;
- Japanese influence = compositional discipline, ink, image mass, line, negative space and materiality rather than novelty iconography;
- wet cotton + crimson absorption = soft/fibrous counter-material to brutalist structure, used without gore.

## 4. Composition grammar

### Prefer
- asymmetric image/text balance;
- one large visual mass rather than many competing illustrations;
- scale contrast;
- overlap where it creates depth;
- strong horizontal/vertical voids;
- image crops that feel intentional rather than centered;
- irregular editorial rhythm;
- occasional full-bleed transition;
- typography-only breathing sections;
- evidence/data shown with restraint, not dashboard styling.

### Reject
- centered hero with headline + subhead + two buttons + floating orb;
- three identical feature cards;
- automatic bento grids;
- equal-width content modules everywhere;
- glass cards as a base aesthetic;
- stacked "section template" rhythm;
- decorative red gradients;
- visual symmetry as default.

## 5. Page narrative

The site should move through **tension → clarity → evidence → resolution**.

### 01 — Arrival
Function: emotional capture + immediate company legibility.

Must answer:
- who is Nexova?
- what world are we entering?
- why continue?

Visual role:
- Oni as dominant mass;
- title and company proposition remain immediately legible;
- movement begins only after comprehension.

### 02 — Manifesto
Function: explain the operating belief.

Treatment:
- quieter;
- more negative space;
- one strong statement;
- no visual overload immediately after hero.

### 03 — Capability Field
Function: present the three current business lines.

Treatment:
- avoid three equal cards;
- use sequencing, stagger, or an editorial field where each capability gets its own spatial weight;
- current-state services only.

### 04 — Evidence
Function: prove maturity.

Allowed evidence:
- founded 2011;
- 120 employees;
- Valencia + Miami;
- three business lines;
- sectors supported by source.

Treatment:
- factual;
- sparse;
- no fake metrics;
- no "dashboard" look.

### 05 — Operating Philosophy
Function: bridge human expertise and modern infrastructure.

Treatment:
- image/text dialogue or typography-led composition;
- future direction may be discussed only as direction, not existing product.

### 06 — Selected Capability Narratives
Function: deepen services without converting page into feature cards.

Treatment:
- 2–3 narrative blocks;
- differentiated layout;
- real company/service language.

### 07 — Presence
Function: geographic/sector grounding.

Treatment:
- calm;
- structural;
- no map gimmick unless it adds real value.

### 08 — Final Declaration
Function: emotional resolution.

Treatment:
- a second strong visual or type moment, lower intensity than hero.

### 09 — CTA
Function: clear next action.

Treatment:
- unambiguous;
- no fake sales urgency.

### 10 — Footer
Function: quiet closure and provenance.

## 6. Hero contract

The hero is the single strongest visual moment.

### Required
- one dominant Oni artwork;
- controlled crop;
- large editorial headline;
- clear company/service proposition;
- no more than one primary CTA plus one secondary text action;
- readable without waiting for animation;
- composition must survive without decorative Japanese text.

### Spatial model
Preferred desktop:
- visual mass occupies ~45–60% of perceived frame;
- text occupies a deliberate counter-mass rather than a conventional column;
- artwork may cross container/grid boundaries;
- negative space isolates headline and CTA.

Preferred mobile:
- artwork becomes a controlled background/upper mass;
- headline sits in front of a calm readable zone;
- ornament reduces materially;
- motion amplitude decreases;
- crop is independently art-directed.

### Signature transition
Hero should become the entry to the next section.

Candidate behavior:
1. Oni enters as stable dominant visual.
2. Scroll begins.
3. foreground atmospheric layer separates subtly.
4. image crop/scale shifts or media window changes.
5. headline releases visual territory.
6. manifesto is revealed.
7. page returns to normal native scroll.

No wheel interception.
No full-page scroll snapping.
No multi-section scroll hijack.

## 7. Typography contract

Typography must carry identity before ornament.

### Roles
**Display:** editorial authority, high contrast or distinctive structure.  
**Body/UI:** restrained, contemporary, high legibility.  
**Utility:** optional mono/condensed layer for metadata only.

### Test directions
1. expressive editorial serif + restrained grotesk;
2. human/calligraphic serif + restrained mono/grotesk;
3. structurally related serif/sans family.

### Rules
- maximum 2 primary families + optional utility face;
- italics may be used as a deliberate accent, not random emphasis;
- uppercase tracking belongs to metadata/utility, not long copy;
- no body copy in ornamental/display face;
- no font purchase before hero comparison proves material value.

## 8. Color contract

Canonical palette remains:
- void black;
- sumi;
- charcoal;
- bone;
- oxblood;
- crimson;
- antique gold.

### Usage
Black/sumi:
- environment / field.

Bone:
- primary copy / contrast.

Oxblood:
- identity signal;
- large controlled fields only when justified.

Crimson:
- action/emphasis;
- not ambient glow.

Gold:
- rare material/accent line;
- never "luxury gold everywhere".

### Forbidden
- cyan;
- neon;
- blue-purple AI gradients;
- chromatic glow;
- saturated multicolor effects.

## 9. Image contract

### Primary
Oni artwork must feel:
- traditional-art-informed;
- physically/materially plausible;
- severe rather than monstrous;
- composition-ready;
- not like fantasy concept art.

### Supporting
Peony:
- mass/foreground framing;
- not decorative wallpaper.

Sumi/smoke:
- depth separation;
- must not obscure copy.

Landscape/moon:
- only when a section needs atmosphere;
- not repeated as motif wallpaper.

### AI-slop rejection
Reject:
- excessive micro-detail with no material logic;
- melted tattoo anatomy;
- over-sharpened generative textures;
- random pseudo-kanji;
- repeated flower anatomy;
- oversaturated red;
- poster composition that leaves no UI safe zone.

## 10. Navigation contract

Navigation behavior stays conventional even if styling is bespoke.

Desktop:
- persistent or near-persistent header;
- clear wordmark;
- concise anchors;
- one clear CTA.

Mobile:
- predictable toggle;
- large targets;
- explicit close state;
- focus management;
- no experimental radial/menu interaction.

Mobbin is reference authority only for interaction sanity, not styling.

## 11. Motion contract

### CSS
- hover/focus;
- small transforms;
- border/surface states.

### Motion
- section entry;
- local image/text reveals;
- nav transitions;
- minor parallax if needed.

### GSAP / ScrollTrigger
Exactly one signature narrative sequence:
- Hero → Manifesto.

### Motion principles
- motion must clarify hierarchy or create narrative continuity;
- no element animates merely because it can;
- content is readable without waiting;
- reduced-motion state lands in the final readable composition;
- native scrolling remains authoritative.

## 12. Materiality contract

Materiality should come from:
- image-level art;
- subtle grain;
- controlled surface contrast;
- restrained sheen;
- line/stroke quality;
- depth/occlusion.

Avoid:
- obvious texture overlays on every surface;
- fake paper panels;
- bevel effects;
- heavy "Japanese parchment" cliché.

## 13. Component strategy

The Golden design is allowed to be compositionally bespoke.

Reusable components are extracted only after repetition appears.

Likely reusable:
- Navigation;
- Button;
- TextLink;
- SectionHeading;
- Divider;
- MediaFrame;
- Capability label/item;
- Footer primitives.

Keep local:
- hero composition;
- signature scroll sequence;
- specific narrative sections.

## 14. Anti-template test

Before H1 approval, hide:
- logo/wordmark;
- company name.

Ask:
1. Does the composition still feel specific?
2. Could this be a crypto/AI/design-agency template?
3. Is Japanese influence structural or just decorative?
4. Is the page memorable because of one authored decision or because of many effects?

If #2 is yes or #3 is decorative, H1 fails.

## 15. Commercial clarity test

Within the first viewport or immediate transition, a reasonable reader should understand:
- Nexova is a mature B2B people/talent/services company;
- it works across recruitment, support and corporate training;
- it is modernizing how it operates.

Mystery may delay detail, not identity.

## 16. Accessibility and performance hierarchy

When visual treatment conflicts with:
1. legibility;
2. focus/keyboard;
3. reduced motion;
4. reasonable performance;

the visual treatment yields.

No exception is granted for "cinematic" intent.

## 17. H1 pass criteria

H1 is approved only if:
- hero reads as authored, not generated/template;
- Oni is integrated into composition;
- company proposition remains legible;
- typography has a deliberate point of view;
- mobile translation is plausible;
- hero-to-manifesto transition has a clear concept;
- decorative Japanese signifiers are unnecessary to make the identity work;
- no generic AI/SaaS structure dominates;
- implementation is feasible in the existing stack.
