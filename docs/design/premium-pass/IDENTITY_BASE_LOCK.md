## Identity thesis

The approved base identity combines:

- brutalist editorial structure;
- traditional Japanese visual language;
- an abstract demon/oni presence fused into darkness rather than rendered as a literal character;
- human-skin-like organic materiality used as substrate, not as visible body imagery;
- wet cotton saturated by crimson liquid as an additional tactile/material motif;
- black, bone, skin-beige, oxblood/crimson, ochre and restrained ash/gold accents;
- cinematic darkness without movie-poster fantasy;
- high contrast, restraint, negative space and controlled visual aggression.

## Functional split

### Brutalism
Controls:
- composition;
- grid;
- typographic scale;
- cropping;
- negative space;
- hard structural relationships.

### Traditional Japanese influence
Controls:
- line;
- ink behavior;
- rhythm;
- print logic;
- asymmetry;
- pigment;
- simplification;
- ornamental restraint.

### Demon / Oni
Controls:
- symbolic identity anchor;
- fragments, eyes, horns, jaw, silhouette and masses;
- should feel partially absorbed by the background / abyss;
- never a complete fantasy character.

### Skin-like materiality
Controls:
- organic matte substrate;
- fine pore/grain;
- pigment absorption;
- warm human material reference;
- never a body/torso/arm subject.

### Crimson-soaked wet cotton
Controls:
- soft fibrous material;
- saturated liquid absorption;
- tension between softness and violence;
- transition/motion/material cue;
- not gore.

## Mandatory visual traits

- demon is abstract, partially dissolved and background-integrated;
- darkness behaves as an abyssal field, not merely a black background;
- skin/cotton materiality remains tactile and controlled;
- crimson is pigment/liquid/accent, not neon glow;
- brutalism is structural;
- Japanese influence is embedded in visual grammar, not cliché iconography;
- typography remains editorial and severe;
- the system must be translatable to a professional B2B website.

## Forbidden outcomes

- tattooed human body as subject;
- fantasy demon;
- ninja/samurai/katana/geisha clichés;
- torii/red-sun/Hokusai-wave dependence;
- cyberpunk/anime/gaming UI;
- generic black/red luxury template;
- movie-poster lighting;
- obvious AI collage/brand-board aesthetics;
- gore;
- excessive decorative kanji;
- ornamental overload.
