## 3. Website purpose

The public website is a **Persuade** surface.

It must:
- communicate Nexova as a mature B2B talent/services company entering a more intelligent operating era;
- make the company legible before it becomes spectacular;
- present the three established lines of business clearly;
- preserve trust, seriousness and human-business credibility;
- feel distinctive enough that it cannot be mistaken for a generic AI/SaaS template;
- support future expansion without pretending that future products already exist.

- dark;
- ceremonial;
- editorial;
- tactile;
- controlled;
- premium;
