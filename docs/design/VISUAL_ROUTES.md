# Nexova Visual Routes — 4g-006

**Status:** CANDIDATE / HUMAN VISUAL LOCK REQUIRED  
**Purpose:** choose one visual language before propagating final website/backoffice design.

All routes must remain:
- accessible and responsive;
- professional B2B rather than generic SaaS-template styling;
- compatible with a public corporate site and a denser internal operations product;
- restrained enough to scale across future Nexova milestones.

## Route A — Precision Human Capital

### Thesis
Nexova as a mature talent consultancy becoming a precise modern operating company.

### Visual language
- dominant warm ivory / paper-white surfaces;
- near-black typography;
- restrained oxblood or burgundy accent;
- editorial typography with a disciplined grotesk/serif pairing;
- generous whitespace, strict grid, thin rules, deliberate asymmetry;
- people/talent photography only when it carries real narrative value.

### Website
Editorial corporate composition: strong service narratives, proof/operating metrics, large typographic statements, restrained imagery, no generic gradient SaaS cards.

### Backoffice
Light operational workspace: dense but calm tables/cards, strong hierarchy, subtle dividers, restrained accent for status/actions.

### Strength
Highest trust/consulting credibility; ages well and gives Nexova a human-business identity.

### Risk
Without distinctive art direction it can become overly safe or resemble a premium consulting template.

---

## Route B — Signal Office

### Thesis
Nexova as a human-operations intelligence company: people, workflows and evidence converted into clear signals.

### Visual language
- deep ink/charcoal foundation;
- bone/ivory text and surfaces;
- restrained crimson signal accent;
- technical typography paired with an editorial display face;
- process lines, evidence marks, status annotations, structured data motifs;
- no neon, cyberpunk glow or decorative "AI" imagery.

### Website
High-contrast storytelling around fragmented operations becoming connected intelligence. Process diagrams and evidence-led service blocks replace generic feature grids.

### Backoffice
An operational "signal room": compact navigation, SLA/status cues, structured panels and explicit source/baseline labels.

### Strength
Most differentiated and most naturally connects Nexova's consulting heritage with AI Engineering and future operational products.

### Risk
If pushed too far it can feel like a cybersecurity/analytics company and underplay the human/talent side.

---

## Route C — Human Systems Atelier

### Thesis
Nexova as an organization-design and talent systems partner: people are the subject, systems are the method.

### Visual language
- limestone / warm off-white;
- graphite;
- muted clay or rust as the single accent;
- highly editorial layouts inspired by organizational reports and cultural journals;
- diagrams based on relationships, teams and process flow rather than dashboards;
- tactile, restrained imagery with documentary rather than stock-SaaS character.

### Website
Human-centered narratives, service journeys, team/process maps and strong editorial sections.

### Backoffice
Warm neutral application with clearer object/relationship views and lower "command-center" intensity than Route B.

### Strength
Most human and brand-distinctive for an HR/talent consultancy; avoids looking like generic AI software.

### Risk
Can under-signal technical sophistication unless the information architecture and data visualization are exceptionally rigorous.

---

## Lock rule
The chosen route becomes the visual source for:
1. golden reference;
2. design tokens;
3. typography;
4. page composition;
5. website components;
6. backoffice component grammar.

Selection does **not** freeze future product architecture. It only locks the current visual system until evidence justifies a redesign.
